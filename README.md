# Nesco's Item Pack
This mod is a small item pack for The Binding of Isaac Repentance that adds 10 items to the game, those being 5 passive items and 5 trinkets.
## Passive Items
### Napster (mostly done / in master)
#### Unlock Requirement: having golden bombs, golden coins, golden hearts and golden Pills unlocked
If Isaac goes to the next floor without taking damage in the boss room, one random pickup will spawn. \
Should Isaac take damage in the boss room, the pickup will have a 50% chance of spawning (this only gets calculated once per floor). \
The possible pickups and their odds are spawning are:
- Golden Bomb -> 15%
- Golden Coin-> 20%
- Golden Pill -> 15%
- Golden Heart -> 15%
- Golden Key -> 20%
- Mega Battery -> 15%
## Trinkets
### Last Supper (partially done / not in master)
Prevents food items from spawning. \
If playing as Judas, Dark Judas or Tainted Judas also gives a 0.5 damage up.
### Monocle (not implemented)
Highlights tinted rocks and cracked skull
